    WHAT WE ARE WORKING TOWARD

    An encounter
    can leave a trace.

    Fear can involve vulnerability, remembered harm and the need to recover. VALOR’s research direction now includes persistent internal needs, a model of its own capability, and experience that changes later behavior.

    Today, the decision core uses known simulation equations. The internal-state observer is an early prototype. Learned threat calibration, memory-guided decisions and evidence of subjective feeling remain open work.
