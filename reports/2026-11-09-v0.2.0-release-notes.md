# VALOR 0.2.0 — research preview

- Explore how two simulated releases share resources, when VALOR uses lookahead or falls back, and what actually happens next.
- Reproduce the frozen paired replication and four shift checks from source. The complete output hashes and paired intervals were verified in a disposable clone.
- Try a read-only SQLite shadow observer that compares proposed and actual changes on a supported disposable workflow. It cannot intervene.

The attached wheel is accompanied by a SHA-256 checksum. See [release instructions](https://github.com/aayups31/valor/blob/v0.2.0/RESEARCH_RELEASE.md) and the [checkpoint report](https://github.com/aayups31/valor/blob/v0.2.0/reports/2026-11-09-build-16.md) for verification and limits.

This prerelease does not establish learned-memory superiority or authorize production execution.
