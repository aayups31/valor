# VALOR landing page and demo · November 15, 2026

The landing page introduces VALOR with an original gold orbital backdrop, a product preview captured from the actual Command Center, and a direct path into the workspace. The supplied screenshots guided the typography, visual tone and placement of the product frame. Command Center is now at `/app` and Visual Studio remains at `/studio`. Opening an instrument from the dashboard and returning still restores the selected run and decision.

The middle section serves a 19.88-second, 1280 × 720 WebM of the After damage simulation. It follows the four proposed options, the selected recovery action, and the recorded health change from 20% to 50%. The ending shows that decision's actual dashboard. The video includes native controls, optional captions, a transcript and a link to replay the example. The asset is 2.46 MB and uses local byte-range delivery for seeking.

The hero reveal, parallax, product frame and film framing use locally packaged GSAP and ScrollTrigger 3.15.1. The scroll effects reverse when scrolling upward and are removed under reduced-motion preferences. Upstream licensing notices are preserved with the distributed files. [GSAP ScrollTrigger documentation](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/ScrollTrigger/).
