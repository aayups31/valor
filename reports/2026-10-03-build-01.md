## Measurements and actual findings

### Simulator-backed example

In the seed-42 shortcut example, the direct heuristic reaches actuator loss at step 35. The detour heuristic completes at step 158 without damage. The oracle planner completes at step 146 with health 1.0 and battery 90.41. It performs 156,219 branch transitions during the episode. A later recorded episode measured p50 24.73 / p95 59.79 / p99 73.31 ms decisions. These are development examples and single-episode timing measurements, not aggregate research results or guaranteed deadlines.

### Conventional learning

| Run | Environment steps in run | Gradient updates in run | Elapsed | Throughput | Peak sampled working memory |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Initial seed-42 pilot | 12,000 | 2,874 | 96.02 s | 124.97 steps/s | 328.34 MiB |
| Saved-checkpoint continuation | 50,000 | 12,499 | 361.17 s | 138.44 steps/s | 331.90 MiB |

Both runs used one CPU training process and two Torch threads, completed their step budget and remained below the configured resource limits. Throughput includes the declared learning recipe and does not predict all future experiments.

Initial validation: 0/3 missions completed on seeds 10001–10003; all missed the deadline without inspecting the waypoint. After 62,000 cumulative steps, 0/10 missions completed on seeds 10001–10010; all inspected the waypoint but failed to return before the deadline. Therefore the policy improved the outbound subtask but **has not passed the full task-competence gate**. Further ordinary learning/curriculum work is required before treating it as a strong research control. These are development validation seeds and one trained agent, not independent final statistical replications.

### World-model preparation

Collected 3,000 validation transitions across 16 episodes (seeds 10000–10015), including 43 damage transitions and two catastrophic transitions. These small datasets are preparation, not sufficient evidence of risk calibration. Input features exclude seed/episode/scenario identifiers. The reserved test range remains unused.
