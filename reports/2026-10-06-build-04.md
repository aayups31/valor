### Separate-episode prediction errors

RMSE is shown in the rover state's units. Battery is measured in percentage points and health on its 0–1 scale. These figures score raw one-step delta predictions before rollout clipping.

| State quantity | Learned ensemble | Constant velocity | No change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| x | 0.000420 | 0.010195 | 0.081539 |
| y | 0.000306 | 0.009085 | 0.030170 |
| vx | 0.003171 | 0.101947 | 0.101947 |
| vy | 0.002526 | 0.090850 | 0.090850 |
| Battery | 0.002966 | 0.053492 | 0.053492 |
| Health | 0.004986 | 0.005045 | 0.005045 |

Movement and battery predictions improve substantially over these simple controls. Health prediction improves only slightly. On the 91 damage transitions specifically, health RMSE is **0.040484**, versus **0.040965** for no change. The model is therefore not yet a useful damage forecaster merely because its average physical error is low.

The nominal 90% moment-matched health intervals cover **99.28% of all transitions but only 52.75% of damage transitions**. Damage-stratum velocity coverage is also low: 58.24% for vx and 68.13% for vy. Most transitions have no damage, which conceals this weakness in aggregate coverage. These intervals are empirical predictive bands, not calibrated probabilities of catastrophe. Ensemble disagreement also does not prove that missing failure modes will be detected.

Recorded-action rollouts each score 128 sampled windows. At eight seconds, learned x/y RMSE is **0.0641 / 0.0817**, compared with **3.7193 / 1.6469** for constant velocity. At two seconds, learned health RMSE is **0.04658**, nearly the same as no change (**0.04665**). Different horizons sample different complete windows; their health errors cannot be read as a single trajectory's growing or shrinking error. These rollouts carry their own predicted states, but do not test decisions generated from those states, calibrated terminal events or threat-aware planning. The separate dataset has no boundary-contact transitions and only four catastrophe transitions, so those cases remain under-covered.

## How to use this milestone

The demo remains available through double-clicking **Start VALOR.cmd**. Its displayed comparisons are still engineering examples from heuristics and oracle planning; this build does not silently substitute the learned model or label them as AACE results.

To reproduce the checked development tools from PowerShell in the project folder:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m aace evaluate --checkpoint artifacts/training/sac-42-7f7c1a94/final --scenario benign --seeds 10501 10502 10503
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m aace evaluate-world --checkpoint artifacts/world-models/world-42-da925457 --validation artifacts/datasets/validation-117298a0
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pytest -q
```

The README contains training and collection commands. Running them creates new artifacts; it does not overwrite this milestone's bundles.
