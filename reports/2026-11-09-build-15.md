# Build report 15 — shared reserves and frozen shift validation

**November 9, 2026.** Checkpoint 8 cleared the simpler-policy route: guarded two-task planning improved decisions in an independent replication. Checkpoint 9 passed the four frozen simulated shift checks for that selected policy. Neither result establishes an advantage from trained memory, subjective fear, or production readiness.

## What changed

Two consecutive releases now share a reserve. Damage, unresolved faults and unused resources carry forward; only the clock and per-task accounting reset. Future stakes are public. The planner can decide that finishing a task now is worth the risk, or preserve capacity for a later task. Costs are charged only when incurred. No policy sees latent danger or future draws.

The unguarded planner maximizes expected score across both tasks. The guarded policy evaluates two complete candidate policies under the same nominal model: two-task lookahead and the unchanged one-task Markov reference. It uses lookahead only for higher predicted score, with expected loss of at most .04 completed tasks and increase of at most .02 failures per pair. Otherwise it runs the baseline. These are screening thresholds on a simulator forecast, not externally authorized risk limits.

The [paired trace](examples/shared-reserve-damaged-v1.json) shows an actual recorded damaged-start case. Both policies completed one high-stakes release. The guarded one repaired and deferred the first release to complete the second faster, scoring 81.5 versus 43.5. Its forecast and the resulting action records are separate. One trace illustrates the behavior; the comparison below measures it.

## Checkpoint 8: independent evidence

The [initial protocol](../01_Architecture/CROSS_TASK_HOLDOUT_PROTOCOL.md) froze the shared-reserve laws, comparisons and four shift cohorts. A guarded arm was added after an engineering-only witness, before any measured run. The [original 512-pair result](2026-11-09-build-15-initial.json) is preserved:

- Unguarded Markov lookahead gained **2.86 score points**, but completion fell by **2.44 percentage points**, failing the completion gate.
- Frozen recurrent lookahead did not beat matched Markov or its reset ablation: mean score differences **+0.080** and **+0.069**, with intervals spanning zero. It also failed the completion gate against myopic Markov. Markov memory did not beat the fixed-risk model at the matched horizon. The report 14 verified-recall findings remain separate.
- Guarded lookahead gained **1.14 score points** and **2.83 completion points**, but the lower score bound was **−0.007**. No initial candidate qualified.

The [replication protocol](../01_Architecture/SHARED_RESERVE_REPLICATION.md) froze an independent comparison of the **unchanged** guarded and myopic policies before opening 2,048 new nominal subjects. No pooling with the initial run, tuning or threshold changes. It passed:

| Metric per paired subject | Guarded | Myopic | Paired difference and 95% interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Campaign score | 68.299 | 65.833 | **+2.466** [1.813, 3.182] |
| Completion per scheduled task | 95.68% | 92.16% | **+3.52 pp** [2.98, 4.08] |
| Terminal failures per scheduled task | 0 observed | 0 observed | 0 observed difference |

Mean shocks were **0.330 vs 0.429**, and propagation incidents **0.125 vs 0.164** per pair. Guarded used lookahead in **30.42%** of cases. It computed **27.95 vs 2.63 new search nodes** per pair with cache reuse, including the screening comparison. This is a planning and screening gain with extra work, not an advantage at matched compute or a win for learned memory. The [selected policy](2026-11-09-build-15-frozen-selection.json) was committed and pushed at `a2ccfdc` before the shift run.

512 fresh paired subjects per scenario, with unchanged forecasts and decision rules. Scores and completion show observed differences; the score lower bound uses the predeclared multiplicity-adjusted interval. The score gate is noninferiority (lower bound ≥ −2), not superiority in every scenario.

Each scenario also passed the frozen completion, failure-difference and aggregate failure-underprediction gates. The highest upper bound on mean failure-count underprediction was **0.0084 per campaign** (damaged start, threshold .08). [Full results](2026-11-09-build-15-shifts.json) include all sixteen bounds, search work and harm metrics. Zero observed failures and degenerate bootstrap bounds do **not** imply zero population risk; these samples cannot rule out rare events. The checks do not cover arbitrary dependency graphs, longer horizons, production distribution shift, or individual risk guarantees. The separate reserved test range was untouched.
