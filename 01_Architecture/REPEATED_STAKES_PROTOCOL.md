# Repeated stakes: checkpoint 7 and 8 development gate

Frozen November 9, 2026 before measured training or comparisons. This is a bounded test of trained predictive state and verified incident retrieval, not subjective fear or production qualification.

## Environment and evidence

Reuse cascade release physics, stakes, and score. A campaign has eight tasks. Time, resource allotment and per-task counters reset; integrity and faults carry over even after stopping or a failed release. Outage is absorbing and all remaining tasks count as uncompleted. Initial regime is equiprobable; between tasks it switches with probability 0.20. It does not change within a task. Public observations are task boundaries and actual fast-action shock/non-shock events. Careful, repair and stop do not manufacture observations. Stakes are 24 or 140 with equal probability. Environment draws are paired by campaign, task and fast-attempt index. No policy reads latent regimes or unused draws.

## Trained state experiment

Train a CPU GRUCell with four hidden units and a linear shock head bounded to [.08,.38]. One-hot tokens denote a boundary, shock or non-shock. Training observes independent public streams from 1,024 campaigns, IDs 100000–101023: each task has 1–3 exogenously sampled fast observations, with no outcome labels besides the next observed shock. Boundary tokens carry no prediction target. Fit seeds 42/43/44, Adam 0.01, 30 epochs, batches of 128 campaigns, two threads. No development-check-driven checkpoint selection or retuning. Frozen weights; only hidden state changes in evaluation.

Compare full within-task search with the trained history model, the same checkpoint reset at every task boundary, a fixed population rate, a four-observation smoothed window (two pseudo-observations at 0.23), and a strong Markov filter. The latter knows the two supplied emission rates, fits its switching probability by maximum observed-stream likelihood on the same training campaigns over {0, .05, .10, .15, .20, .25, .30, .40, .50}, then freezes it. Each predictor updates on hypothetical observations during search. The planner optimizes one task at a time; it does not claim campaign-optimal foresight.

## Verified incident experiment

Build a bounded shared evidence bank from 256 separate exploratory campaigns (102000–102255), acting uniformly over legal non-stop actions when possible. At each action, use the frozen Markov model to choose contingent within-task continuations. Audit the taken action and each alternative by integrating those fixed continuations at both declared danger endpoints. Keep a recourse only if its score improves by at least one point at both endpoints without raising terminal failure probability at either. A record separates the observed transition and actual eventual score from model-derived branches. Scope includes exact public release state, stakes, model version and in-scope regime assumptions. Keep the last 256 distinct contexts in encounter order, replacing duplicate context entries. Log branch evaluations; all retrieval arms share this offline work and bank. Do not update it during checks.

Compare a two-non-stop-candidate shortlist (plus stop) using the same Markov predictor and continuation search across arms: heuristic order, factual recall of a completed task's taken action in a matching context, and verified recall that prioritizes a qualified alternative and otherwise uses factual recall. The heuristic prioritizes repair with faults, then fast for high stakes or careful for ordinary stakes, then the other processing action, then repair. Recall never changes physics, legal actions, forecasts or objective and the proposed action is scored again. Full Markov search is a separate unconstrained-root reference. Count candidate and continuation work separately; sharing a root cap does not imply identical compute.

## Measurement and gates

Use 256 fresh paired campaigns (110000–110255); engineering smoke uses separate IDs and small fits. Primary predeclared differences: mean over three trained fits versus Markov (and versus their matched resets); verified versus factual under the same root cap. Report score, completion per scheduled task, outages, release failures, shocks, propagation, repair, stops, predictions on observed attempts, memory hits, and computation. Use paired campaign bootstrap intervals; fits are not independent campaign replicates. Evidence of benefit requires a positive lower interval bound on score, completion loss no worse than 0.02 and failure increase no greater than 0.01 using corresponding interval endpoints. Report negative, inconclusive and sparse-coverage results. This is an in-distribution development gate; final validation and workflow qualification remain separate.
