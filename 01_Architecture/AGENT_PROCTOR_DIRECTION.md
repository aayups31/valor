# VALOR as an agent proctor

Owner-confirmed October 12, 2026. This extends the research/product direction; it does not assert production readiness or subjective feelings.

VALOR should supervise consequential work proposed by another AI agent. The worker supplies a public task state and finite proposed actions. VALOR estimates consequences, considers vulnerability, prior harm and recovery, and assesses alternatives under fixed human permissions. External authority remains responsible for application and human stop. Protection concerns the authorized task and its resources, not resistance to shutdown.

Fear plus logic means learned anticipation and persistent harmful experience can direct scrutiny and protective proposals, while explicit objectives, uncertainty and constraints determine whether proceeding is justified. Unnecessary abandonment is a failure. A useful mechanism must outperform serious simpler controls with declared evidence and work budgets.

The experience should let a novice inspect what VALOR anticipates: evaluated branches, predicted changes, possible damage, recovery requirements, uncertainty, selected/rejected reasons and eventual observed outcomes. Structured digital consequences can be shown as state timelines; physical trajectories can be drawn when the model supports them. Previews are bounded model predictions, not a view of the future, a transcript of private thoughts, or an exhaustive search of possibilities. Missing/unexplored evidence remains visible.

Preserve [the premium interface direction](../UI_DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md) and [the existing experience contract](PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE_DIRECTION.md). Prefer task outcomes and plain contextual explanations over technical dashboards or emotional percentages.

Sequence: scoped action-conditioned prediction and qualification; offline previews; shadow integration with a bounded agent workflow; audited authority integration only after evidence and permissions pass. The open-source research toolkit can later support an SDK and managed workflow integrations if measured utility and customer demand justify them. Current work controls simulations only; this is not a general safety certificate.
