# Learned stakes, vulnerability and cascading consequences

Owner clarification recorded October 12, 2026. The learned mechanism is central to VALOR; an external approval wrapper alone does not fulfill the aim.

Investigate persistent internal needs and experience-dependent protective state. Harm must affect actual future capability, recovery costs and options. Anticipated loss can alter learned attention, proposal ordering and learning, while explicit logic pursues the authorized task under fixed human permissions. The interests being protected include task resources and affected people/systems, not merely model uptime or avoidance of shutdown.

Extend the world-model task toward temporal dependencies and cascading effects: a local action changes downstream state; later actions can amplify or contain damage; recovery can become harder. Show only evaluated, supported branches and their uncertainty. Long-horizon inference can compound errors, so the scope/horizon and unsupported dependencies must stay visible.

Test learned sensitivity to stakes against conventional risk, history, retrieval, replay and stronger planning under matched evidence/work. Compare the same capable worker with native protections, improved prompting, conventional supervision and VALOR; also test whether a stronger worker alone gives better workflow value. Customer demand and repeated adoption are separate from a positive benchmark.

Fear and survival are computational motivational mechanisms to investigate. Causal behavioral effects can be tested; subjective feeling cannot be established by naming a state, defensive behavior or a model's self-report. Existing intrinsic-fear, homeostatic and world-model research supplies precedents, not validation of VALOR's proposed combination.

Sequence: coherent consequences; trained state/needs with meaningful loss and recovery; bounded cascading task environment; matched causal ablations; qualified incident memory; scoped agent supervision and readable previews. Keep [the original research controls](../IMPLEMENTATION_MASTER_PLAN.md), [the proctor direction](AGENT_PROCTOR_DIRECTION.md) and premium product principles. Present negative results rather than equating a large architecture or polished preview with strength.
